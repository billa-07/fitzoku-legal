Delete Your Account and Data

Operated by Rajpoot Holding LLC  ·  Last updated: [05/09/2026]

You can delete your Fitzoku account and all of the personal data associated with it at any time. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Fitzoku and go to the Profile tab
  2. Scroll to Settings
  3. Select Delete account
  4. Read the confirmation screen and type DELETE to confirm

Your account and data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or cannot sign in, email us at [INSERT SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Delete my account".

We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days, and confirm by email when it is done.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Encrypted backups: residual copies may persist in our infrastructure provider's encrypted backups for up to 90 days before being overwritten.
  • Transaction records: records of purchases are retained as long as required by tax and accounting law, generally up to 7 years. These contain purchase identifiers, not your fitness data.
  • Anonymised analytics: aggregate usage statistics that cannot be linked back to you are not deleted, as they contain no personal information.
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, your training history, progress photos and personal records cannot be recovered. Consider exporting your data first — Settings → Data → Export my data.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Cancel it separately in your app store account settings, otherwise billing will continue.

  • Google Play: Play Store → profile icon → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions
  • Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions

Guest accounts

If you used Fitzoku without creating an account, your data is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. Nothing is held on our servers that can identify you.
