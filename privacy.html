1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Rajpoot Holding LLC, a limited liability company registered in Wyoming, United States ("Rajpoot Holding," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Fitzoku mobile application (the "App").

We are the data controller for the personal information described here.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

Account information

  • Email address and password (passwords are stored only as salted hashes by our authentication provider — we never see or store your plaintext password)
  • Sign-in identifiers if you use Google or Apple sign-in

Profile and fitness information

  • Name, age, gender, height, and weight
  • Body type and body shape assessment answers
  • Fitness goals, experience level, available equipment, and weekly schedule
  • Injuries or physical conditions you choose to disclose
  • Free-text notes you write for the AI trainer
  • Language, units, and other preferences

Activity information

  • Workout logs: exercises performed, sets, repetitions, weights, duration, and dates
  • Personal records and achievements
  • Body weight entries and body measurements over time
  • Mood and energy ratings you optionally record
  • Favorited exercises and search activity within the App

Progress photos

  • Photos you choose to capture or upload to track physical changes

AI trainer conversations

  • Messages you send to the AI trainer and the responses generated

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system version, app version, language and region settings, and a device or installation identifier
  • Usage data: screens viewed, features used, session length and frequency, and interaction events
  • Diagnostic data: crash reports, error logs, and performance metrics
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address for security, fraud prevention, and regional settings. The App does not collect precise GPS location.

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, microphone, or messages.
  • We do not read your device's photo library beyond the specific images you select.
  • We do not connect to health platforms such as Apple Health or Google Fit unless and until you explicitly enable such a feature in a future version.

3. Sensitive Information

Some information you provide — body measurements, weight, injuries, progress photos, and physical condition notes — may be considered health-related or sensitive personal data in jurisdictions such as the European Union, United Kingdom, California, and South Korea.

  • We process this information only to provide the App's core functionality to you: generating your workout plans, tracking your progress, and enabling the AI trainer to respond to your circumstances.
  • Where the law requires consent for processing sensitive data, we rely on the explicit consent you give by choosing to enter this information. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting the information or your account.
  • We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Providing injury, health, or photo information is always optional. The App functions without it, though plan personalization will be less precise.

5. AI Processing

When you use plan generation or the AI trainer, we transmit relevant context to a third-party large language model provider so it can generate a response. This may include your profile, goals, equipment, injuries you disclosed, workout history, the free-text notes you wrote, and your chat messages.

  • Progress photos are never sent to AI providers.
  • We instruct our AI provider not to use data submitted through our account to train their models.
  • We send only what is needed to generate a useful response.
  • AI output may be inaccurate and is not medical advice — see our Terms of Use, Section 3.

Please avoid entering medical record details, government identifiers, or other people's personal information into the AI trainer.

8. Data Retention

  • Account and profile data: retained while your account is active
  • Workout logs, progress entries, and measurements: retained while your account is active, so your history remains available to you
  • Progress photos: retained until you delete them or delete your account
  • AI trainer conversations: retained while your account is active, so the trainer has continuity; you may clear them

  • Analytics data: typically retained up to 24 months in pseudonymized form

When you delete your account, we delete your personal data from our active systems, including your photos, promptly and in any case within 30 days. Residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before being overwritten. We may retain limited records where legally required.

10. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information:

  • All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS
  • Data is encrypted at rest by our infrastructure providers
  • Authentication tokens are stored in the device's secure enclave (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore)
  • Row-level security ensures each user can access only their own records
  • Progress photos are stored in a private bucket and served only via short-lived signed URLs. They are never publicly accessible.
  • Location metadata (EXIF) is stripped from photos before upload
  • API credentials for third-party services are held server-side and never included in the App

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the App at your own risk. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.
